Why we exist

We started CryptoCube because we were frustrated. Frustrated with exchanges that felt like they were built for robots, not humans. Frustrated with hidden fees that ate into every trade. Frustrated with customer support that took days to respond.

So we built something different. A platform that prioritizes clarity over complexity, transparency over trickery, and speed over everything else.

Our goal isn't to be the biggest exchange. It's to be the one you actually enjoy using.

Speed first

Settlement in under 2 minutes. Because your time matters.

Radical transparency

Every fee shown upfront. No surprises, ever.

Security by default

Cold storage, 2FA, and real-time threat monitoring.

Human support

Real people, 24/7. Average response time: 12 minutes.

Our journey

From idea to 50,000 users in 18 months

January 2025
CryptoCube is founded
Three former fintech engineers leave their jobs to build a better crypto exchange. The first commit is pushed from a coffee shop in Berlin.
March 2025
Beta launch
1,000 beta testers get early access. The feedback is brutal but invaluable. We rebuild the UI from scratch in three weeks.
June 2025
Public launch
CryptoCube goes live to the public. 10,000 users sign up in the first week. The servers hold up. Barely.
September 2025
$1B volume milestone
We process our billionth dollar in trading volume. The team celebrates with pizza and immediately gets back to work.
December 2025
50,000 users
We hit 50,000 active traders. Mobile apps are in development. Regulatory licenses pending in EU and UK.
2026
The future
Staking, margin trading, and institutional accounts coming soon. The journey is just beginning.
